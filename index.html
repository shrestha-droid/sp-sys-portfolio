About

I build software where AI, systems design and the web meet, and I care most about the parts that have to be correct: security, simulation, and anything that has to keep working when nobody is watching.

This year that has meant a desktop app that turns a sentence into a running full-stack app, a client-side vault that splits secrets between people you trust, a crowd-safety simulator for mass gatherings, and several NASA Space Apps entries. I'm also the founder of Concierge, where I build full-stack and AI software.
